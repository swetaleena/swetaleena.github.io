Featured product: Scrum Buddy

A multi-agent AI platform for Agile learning

Business schools wanted students to experience an industry-like Agile team without needing an experienced professional in every session. I led product definition for a platform where AI agents play the Business Analyst, Scrum Master, QA Engineer and Product Owner.

  • Defined the MVP and product workflow, and co-authored the PRD
  • Designed persona switching, the AI evaluation framework and the prompt strategy
  • Shaped features such as the BA Story Optimizer, QA Edge Case Generator and Retro Facilitator

Reported outcomes: 40% less effort to write user stories, 15% higher sprint completion and about 3.5 hours saved per team each week.
